How it works

Fosbury reads the part you don’t have time for.

01

Open to a queue, not a river.

Everything waiting on you, in one list, ordered by what actually matters. Each thread carries an owner, a priority and a state — so you are reading a to-do list somebody already sorted, not nine hundred messages in the order they happened to arrive.

02

Ask, instead of scrolling back.

Every decision your company has made is written down somewhere in here. Ask for it in a sentence and the agent reads the threads, checks nothing has superseded it, and tells you where it was settled — with the receipt attached.

03

Hand off the chasing.

The thing you cannot finish now is the thing that rots. Ask once, then tell the agent what to do if nobody answers — it will go back into the thread, ask again in your words, and close it when it has what you needed.

Most of the work went into what we refused to build.

Every switch above is one somebody asked us for. Presence, receipts, badges — each is a small way of paying people to sit in the app, and a tool for teams that build cannot afford any of them. Nothing in Fosbury assumes you are online, rewards you for being online, or punishes you for being away.
