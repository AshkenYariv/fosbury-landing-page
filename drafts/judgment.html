Fosbury

Everything got cheap except judgement.

Context, drafts, summaries, replies — a machine will make your team as much of all of it as you like, for nothing, forever. The one input a company still cannot manufacture is somebody deciding. Fosbury is built to spend that carefully, and to keep what it produces.

The Fosbury decision record  
001Closed4 MarchYariv

Public channels only.

Private channels are where context goes to die. We took the feature out rather than ship it and spend three years apologising for it. It cost us two deals in the first month.

David Kim

Every team we talk to asks for private channels in the first ten minutes. We are going to lose deals over this.

Elena Ruiz

They ask for it because Slack taught them to. What they actually want is not to be embarrassed in front of the whole company, and that is a smaller problem with a smaller answer.

David Kim

DMs cover the embarrassment case. Fine. But we should say the reason out loud rather than let people discover the hole.

Yariv · decided

Public only, and we say why on the website. If we are wrong we will find out from people who left, and that is a cheaper way to learn it than shipping the thing we think is wrong.

002Closed19 MarchElena Ruiz

Nothing rewards you for being online.

No presence dots, no typing indicators, no read receipts, no “active now”. Every one of them is a way of paying people to sit in the app, and we would rather not be able to afford that.

Priya Nair

Read receipts are the single most requested thing from managers we demo to.

Elena Ruiz

That is the argument against them, not for them. A read receipt is a manager’s tool paid for out of an engineer’s afternoon.

Elena Ruiz · decided

None of them. If a thread needs an answer it has an owner and a due time, which is a real mechanism. Presence is not a mechanism, it is a mood.

003Closed2 AprilYariv

Every conversation has to be able to end.

A thread carries a state and an owner and can be closed, the way a task can. It turns out this is the whole difference: a stream you can only fall behind on, versus a queue you can finish.

Marco Bianchi

Closing a conversation feels wrong. Conversations do not close, people just stop talking.

Sarah Chen

That is exactly the problem. “People stopped talking” is indistinguishable from “nobody decided”, and six weeks later nobody can tell you which one happened.

Yariv · decided

Threads close. What we are actually selling is the difference between a conversation that stopped and a conversation that finished.

004Closed21 AprilPriya Nair

The agent is a member, not a feature.

It has a name, a face and a seat in the room. It gets assigned threads, it chases the people who owe answers, and it closes what it finishes. It is not a sidebar you open, and it is not a button called “Summarise”.

Priya Nair

If it sits in a panel it becomes something you remember to use, which means most days nobody uses it.

David Kim

Giving it an avatar is going to read as a gimmick to some people.

Priya Nair · decided

It reads as a gimmick until the first time it closes a thread you forgot about. It is a member. The whole point is that the reading is done by something that does not get tired, so that the deciding is left to something that does.

005Closed8 MaySarah Chen

No loading state, anywhere.

Not a spinner, not a skeleton, not a shimmer. If a screen cannot be drawn instantly it is the wrong screen. You will open this thing forty times a day, and the tax is charged forty times.

Marco Bianchi

A skeleton screen is not a loading state, it is a courtesy.

Sarah Chen · decided

A skeleton screen is an apology rendered at sixty frames a second. Ban them and the architecture arguments resolve themselves, because there is nowhere left to hide the latency.

006Closed16 MayYariv

We took the name from a high jumper.

In 1968 Dick Fosbury won Olympic gold by going over the bar backwards while everyone else was training harder at the same jump. Within ten years, nobody jumped the old way. Not a faster stream. A different one.

Elena Ruiz

Nobody under forty knows who he is. We will spend the first sentence of every conversation explaining the name.

Yariv · decided

Then the first sentence of every conversation is about doing the obvious thing backwards, which is the only sentence I want to be having. Keep it.
