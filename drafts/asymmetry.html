Writing got free. Reading didn’t.

Every tool your team uses was built when a paragraph cost an hour. That stopped being true about eighteen months ago.

The arithmetic

A message costs the person who sends it thirty seconds and the room that reads it thirty minutes. Chat never charged anybody for that, so volume only ever moved one way. Now that a thousand words can be produced for nothing, the bill has come due.

In today’s rapidly evolving digital workplace, organizations across every vertical are increasingly discovering that effective internal communication is more mission-critical than it has ever been before. As distributed and hybrid teams continue to scale at an unprecedented pace, the challenges of maintaining alignment, driving meaningful cross-functional collaboration, and ensuring that key stakeholders remain informed and engaged have never been more pressing or more consequential to enterprise outcomes.

Industry research consistently demonstrates that today’s knowledge workers spend a significant and growing portion of their day managing, triaging, and responding to internal communications — valuable time that could otherwise be redirected toward the high-impact, revenue-generating activities that genuinely move the needle for the business and unlock durable competitive advantage in the marketplace.

It has therefore become abundantly clear to forward-thinking leaders that a fundamental rethinking of how modern teams communicate is no longer merely a nice-to-have or a discretionary line item, but rather a strategic imperative for any organization that is serious about unlocking its full potential, future-proofing its operating model, and thriving in an increasingly competitive and fast-moving landscape.

What we changed

The sender pays, not the room.

Every conversation has an owner, so it is somebody’s.

Every conversation has an end, so it stops.

Agents read all of it, so you read almost none.

Fosbury delivers a comprehensive, best-in-class suite of enterprise-grade communication capabilities purpose-built from the ground up to empower modern product organizations to collaborate more effectively, more intelligently, and more asynchronously than ever before. Our robust ownership framework ensures that every single conversation is assigned to a clearly designated accountable party, driving unprecedented levels of transparency and accountability across the entire organizational surface area.

Meanwhile, our powerful and highly configurable resolution workflows enable teams to seamlessly move conversations through a fully customizable lifecycle from open to resolved to closed, while our next-generation AI-powered intelligent agent layer leverages state-of-the-art large language models to automatically surface, summarize, prioritize and action the signal hiding within the noise — so your team can focus on what truly matters most.

Whether you are a fast-growing seed-stage startup or a global enterprise operating at massive scale, Fosbury flexibly adapts to your unique workflows and meets your organization exactly where it is on its communication maturity journey.

The name

In 1968 Dick Fosbury won Olympic gold by going over the bar backwards. Everyone else was training harder at the same jump. Within ten years, nobody jumped the old way.

Our brand identity draws profound inspiration from the remarkable and inspiring story of Richard Douglas “Dick” Fosbury, the visionary American athlete and true category-defining disruptor who fundamentally reimagined the high jump at the 1968 Summer Olympic Games in Mexico City by pioneering an entirely novel and initially controversial back-first technique that would come to be known throughout the athletics world as the Fosbury Flop.

Much as Fosbury himself refused to accept the prevailing conventional wisdom of his era and instead chose to challenge the status quo through relentless first-principles thinking, we too are on an ambitious mission to fundamentally reimagine what workplace communication can and should be for the next generation of high-performing teams. We believe, deeply and sincerely, that incremental optimization of a broken paradigm can only ever yield incremental results.
