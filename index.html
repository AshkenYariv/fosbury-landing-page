Introducing Fosbury

The more enjoyable, more efficient way for product teams to communicate.

How a team communicates decides how fast it moves and whether the work is any good. Yet the leading tools were designed over a decade ago, for an office where everyone sat in the same room on the same clock — one endless stream, everyone present, newest at the bottom.

It is rare to find a top product company that truly works asynchronously, yet nearly all of them describe themselves that way. The app is still open all day and the pings still arrive.

01Speed and zero friction. Keyboard-first, and instant.

02Structure by default. Every thread has an owner, a state and an end.

03Async by default. Nothing rewards you for being online.

The name is borrowed. In 1968 Dick Fosbury went over the bar backwards while everyone else trained harder at the same jump. Within ten years nobody jumped the old way. Not a faster stream. A different one.
